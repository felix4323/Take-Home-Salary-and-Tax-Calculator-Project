# Take-Home Salary and Tax Calculator Project

Python project that calculates take-home pay and analyses how the proportion of gross income paid in Income Tax, National Insurance, and loan repayments changes across different salary levels.

## Overview

This project calculates UK take-home salary by incorporating Income Tax, National Insurance, and Student Loan and Postgraduate Loan repayments. It analyses how the proportion of gross income paid in each category changes across different salary levels.

The project also compares UK take-home pay with US federal and New York State/City taxes across a range of income levels.

## Key Features

- Calculates UK Income Tax, National Insurance and Student Loan repayments.
- Compares take-home salary with and without Student Loan repayments.
- Includes Postgraduate Loan repayments.
- Calculates and visualises marginal and effective tax rates across different salary levels.
- Visualises the proportion of gross salary allocated to Income Tax, National Insurance, Student Loan repayments and take-home income.
- Compares UK take-home pay with US federal and New York State/City taxation.
